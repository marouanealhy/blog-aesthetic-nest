Dedicated dressing rooms are wonderful, but you don't need a spare bedroom to experience that bespoke, boutique shopping feel every morning.

The **wardrobe-vanity combo**—carving out a recessed dressing niche right between two floor-to-ceiling wardrobes—has become one of the biggest luxury interior trends of the year.

Here is how to design a vanity nook that maximizes vertical square footage while looking completely custom.

---

## 1. The Power of the Built-In Niche

Instead of running continuous closet doors along an entire wall, break the run by dedicating a 36-to-48-inch central span for a floating vanity counter.

This break creates depth, opens up sightlines across the bedroom, and gives you a dedicated station for skincare, makeup, and jewelry.

## 2. Upgrade the Mirror: Backlit Arch Magic

Lighting in a recessed closet nook can easily feel cavernous. The solution is an **arched backlit LED mirror**. The curved top softens the rigid grid of closet cabinetry, while the diffused 3000K glow eliminates facial shadows.

## 3. Tuck-Away Seating with Hidden Storage

Avoid chairs with high backs that block access to adjacent wardrobe doors. A low-profile, round ribbed velvet ottoman slides completely underneath your desk counter when not in use.

## 4. Curate Your Perfume & Everyday Jewels

Rather than hiding your favorite fragrance bottles, display them like museum pieces on a tiered ribbed acrylic riser or a fluted marble tray.

> *"When your daily essentials are curated with intention, getting dressed in the morning stops feeling like a chore and transforms into a grounding ritual."*

### Pro Tip for Small Apartments
If you are working with an IKEA Pax or standard modular closet system, you can easily replicate this look by omitting the center wardrobe doors, sliding a floating drawer insert into the lower half, and mounting an arched mirror above!
